RoFront [World PvP] is a Roblox strategy experience by Torrey Pines built around real-time territory conquest. According to its official Roblox page, it is based on the Open Front game and credits Open Front for the map. You begin with a single nation, expand outward, attack neighbors, and compete against other nations and tribes for control of the world. The published victory condition is to conquer 80% of the world.

That description is the authority for what RoFront advertises. It is not a soldier-first-person shooter, and it is not a class-based combat game. Understanding RoFront mechanics starts with treating it as a map-based conquest game where borders, buildings, diplomacy, and the world-control meter matter more than any individual firefight.

## The Core Loop: Expand, Attack, Control

The official description lists the main actions: expand from a single nation, attack your neighbors, build cities, ports, and factories, forge alliances, and launch nukes. The win condition is conquering 80% of the world.

From a territory conquest premise, the practical implication is that your first decisions should be about position, not aggression. Before committing to an attack, identify where your territory sits, who shares your borders, and how the world-control display reads. A single successful push can improve your position, but it does not complete the match on its own. The 80% goal is a match-long target, so early moves should set up later ones rather than exhaust you immediately.

This is strategic advice inferred from the conquest premise, not a measured mechanic. The official listing does not publish attack formulas, expansion rates, or timing windows, so any specific "always attack at X%" claim should be treated as unverified.
