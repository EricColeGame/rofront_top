## Core Gameplay Mechanics and Movement Fundamentals

RoFront demands deliberate movement and calculated pacing rather than reckless sprinting across open terrain. The physics model penalizes erratic maneuvers, meaning players who expose themselves in no-man's-land without smoke concealment or covering fire quickly end up back in the deployment screen. 

Movement speed depends directly on the weapon you carry and your stamina bar. When your stamina depletes, your soldier breathes heavily, weapon sway dramatically increases, and aim-down-sights (ADS) transitions slow to a crawl. Player experience indicates that pacing your advances between hard cover points preserves stamina for sudden firefights.

Suppression is another core system that defines combat encounters. When high-caliber rounds land near your position, your peripheral vision blurs, screen shake intensifies, and weapon recoil jumps significantly. Learning to stay calm under fire and return burst fire allows you to break suppression and return accurate shots.

| Action / Mechanic | Primary Control | Tactical Function | Performance Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sprint** | Left Shift | Rapid repositioning between cover | Never sprint with less than 25% stamina remaining. |
| **Crouch** | C / Left Ctrl | Lowers profile and tightens bullet spread | Use behind low trench parapets and sandbags. |
| **Prone** | Z / Double-tap C | Maximizes stability, minimizes hit box | High vulnerability to flanking grenades; keep an exit open. |
| **Mount / Lean** | Q / E (Contextual) | Peeking corners while shielding torso | Lean from the right side of cover to minimize body exposure. |
| **Interact / Bandage** | F / Hotkey 4 | Revives, fixes guns, applies tourniquets | Always bandage inside deep cover; bleeding slows sprint speed. |

Mastering the physical flow highlighted in this RoFront beginner guide ensures that you never get caught flat-footed while reloading or catching your breath between trench lines.

## Performance Settings and Hardware Optimization

Achieving smooth framerates is critical for landing precise shots during intense close-quarters confrontations. Complex smoke particles, mud physics, and sustained explosions can tax hardware and introduce input latency.

Tuning your client according to this RoFront beginner guide will stabilize your frame times and sharpen competitive clarity:

1. **Field of View (FOV):** Set between 85 and 95 degrees. While higher values provide wider peripheral sight, they compress distant targets into tiny targets, complicating long-range rifle duels.
2. **Camera Shake & Motion Blur:** Turn off motion blur and dial camera shake down to minimum allowed levels. This eliminates screen distortion when artillery impacts nearby.
3. **Shadow and Particle Quality:** Lower particle density to see clearly through artillery dust clouds while maintaining high render distance for environmental geometry.
4. **Mouse Sensitivity:** Lower your overall aim sensitivity. High DPI settings cause erratic over-aiming; a consistent, lower sensitivity builds reliable muscle memory for tracking sprinting targets.

Adjusting these settings ensures you experience consistent responsiveness when every split-second trigger pull counts.

## RoFront Beginner Guide: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most effective starter weapon for new players?
For players just starting their journey, standard bolt-action service rifles like the default infantry rifle offer the best balance of range, base damage, and ammo efficiency. They force you to develop trigger discipline, precision aiming, and cover utilization before transitioning to rapid-firing submachine guns or heavy machine guns.

### Why is this RoFront beginner guide recommending the Rifleman class first?
The Rifleman provides straightforward mechanics with dependable stopping power at mid-to-long range without demanding micromanagement of specialist equipment. Starting with the Rifleman allows newcomers to learn map layouts, bullet velocity, and squad positioning without the heavy responsibilities carried by Medics or Support Gunners.

### How do I stop bleeding out after taking damage?
When hit by gunfire or shrapnel, your health bar drains gradually if bleeding status triggers. Take cover immediately and press your interact/bandage key (typically keybind F or hotkey 4). You cannot sprint or fire while applying medical bandages, so ensure you are completely concealed behind solid cover before beginning the healing animation.

### How does weapon mounting work with Light Machine Guns?
When standing, crouching, or lying prone near an appropriate flat surface—such as sandbag barriers, window ledges, or trench rims—look directly at the ledge and press your mount key. Mounting clamps the bipod down, reducing weapon recoil and eliminating weapon sway for accurate sustained suppression fire.
