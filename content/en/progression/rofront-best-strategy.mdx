## Start by Reading the Map, Not by Attacking

The most common early mistake in any territory conquest game is expanding before you understand your position. In RoFront, your first minutes should be spent identifying three things:

- Where your borders are and which neighbors touch them
- What the world-control display shows, since that is the progress meter toward the advertised 80% goal
- Which nearby nations look exposed versus which look like they will punish an overextension

## Expansion Is a Progression System, Not a Single Decision

Progression in RoFront runs through territory. Every nation you absorb or push back changes your borders, your exposure, and your position on the world-control meter. That means expansion should be sequenced rather than constant.

A practical way to think about it:

| Phase | Reasonable focus | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Secure a defensible border and learn the interface | You have the least information and the most to lose |
| Mid | Take neighbors who cannot retaliate effectively | Growth compounds, but only if your flanks hold |
| Late | Push toward the 80% threshold while managing threats | The goal is world control, not a single won fight |

The official description says you attack your neighbors, which implies adjacency matters. It does not say whether attacks require a specific resource, cooldown, or unit count. Check the in-game controls before committing, and do not assume a number you saw in a video applies to your match.
