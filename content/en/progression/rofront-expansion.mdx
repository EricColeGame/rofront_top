## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most effective squad layout for a RoFront expansion push?
A well-balanced fireteam consists of one squad leader for coordination, two assault infantry for clearing hostiles, one combat engineer for building cover, one support gunner for suppressive fire, and one field medic to sustain the team. This balance ensures your unit has the firepower to breach enemy sectors and the logistical tools to reinforce the newly captured ground.

### How does supply logistics impact a RoFront expansion?
Logistics dictates how long a squad can hold ground once an initial push succeeds. Without a continuous line of ammunition, medical kits, and building supplies, soldiers quickly run out of resources, leaving them vulnerable to counter-offensives. Establishing supply caches and keeping transit routes clear ensures your frontline remains well-equipped during prolonged sieges.

### Why do early-game offensive pushes frequently fail?
Early pushes often fail because players rush into enemy sectors without securing their rear flanks or constructing forward spawn points. If advancing soldiers are eliminated far from friendly lines, the long run back creates defensive gaps that hostile forces can exploit to regain lost territory.

### When should engineers build concrete bunkers instead of sandbag redoubts?
Sandbag redoubts are ideal for immediate cover during an active firefight because they can be built quickly. Concrete bunkers should be prioritized once the immediate perimeter is clear and your squad needs to establish a permanent defensive anchor that can withstand heavy artillery, sniper fire, and sustained counter-attacks.
