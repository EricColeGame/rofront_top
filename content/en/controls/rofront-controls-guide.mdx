## Optimal Sensitivity and Control Settings for Competitive Play

Fine-tuning hardware configurations alongside this **RoFront controls guide** will elevate your target acquisition speed and tracking accuracy. Running an excessively high sensitivity causes erratic crosshair bounce during micro-adjustments, particularly at extreme combat ranges.

Most tactical FPS experts recommend calibrating your mouse for consistent centimeter-per-360 tracking, prioritizing stable control over frantic twitches.

| Target Playstyle | Recommended DPI | In-Game Sensitivity | Effective eDPI | Primary Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frontline Breacher** | 800 | 0.25 – 0.35 | 200 – 280 | Rapid target acquisition during trench sweeps |
| **Support Gunner (LMG)**| 800 | 0.20 – 0.28 | 160 – 224 | Smooth tracking for sustained suppressive fire |
| **Long-Range Sniper** | 400 – 800 | 0.15 – 0.22 | 120 – 176 | Pixel-perfect micro-adjustments on distant targets |
| **All-Around Rifleman** | 800 | 0.22 – 0.30 | 176 – 240 | Balanced recoil control and perimeter awareness |

To improve your personal performance, implement these system-level adjustments:

- **Disable Windows Pointer Precision:** Navigate to your operating system mouse properties and turn off "Enhance pointer precision" to prevent hardware-level mouse acceleration.
- **Set Lean Keys to Toggle or Rebind:** If pressing `Q` and `E` interferes with your strafing fingers (`A` and `D`), consider binding lean functions to secondary mouse thumb buttons (`Mouse 4` and `Mouse 5`).
- **Optimize Field of View (FOV):** Maintain an FOV between 85 and 95 degrees. Overly wide FOVs cause a fisheye effect that makes distant targets appear unnaturally tiny.
- **Audio Output Calibration:** Set sound to stereo headphones with software spatial surround disabled to accurately pinpoint footsteps and directional shell casings.

Through deliberate muscle memory training and tailored sensitivities, your responses will become second nature during contested sector battles.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I rebind my keys directly within the game?
Yes, access the main settings menu by pressing `Esc` or navigating to the options gear on the home interface. From there, select the Controls tab to customize your primary and secondary key mappings to match your personal ergonomic preferences.

### Why should I customize my layout beyond the standard RoFront controls guide?
While the default layout outlined in this **RoFront controls guide** suits most players, personal hand size, mechanical keyboard layouts, and multi-button gaming mice offer custom advantages. Offloading lean or melee inputs to mouse thumb buttons keeps your movement fingers firmly planted on `WASD`.

### Does RoFront support gamepads and flight sticks?
Basic gamepad support is detected automatically by the platform, allowing controller players to move and shoot using analog sticks and triggers. However, precision long-distance combat remains significantly easier on keyboard and mouse due to the lack of aggressive aim assist mechanics.

### How do I fix input lag or delayed key response?
Input latency usually results from running uncapped frame rates alongside vertical sync (V-Sync). Disable V-Sync in the graphics options, ensure your monitor refresh rate is set to its highest setting in your display drivers, and lower ambient shadow rendering to maintain stable, high frame rates.
